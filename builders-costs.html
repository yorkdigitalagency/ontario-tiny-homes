The all-in cost stack

  • Home package: $39,000–$320,000 depending on size, finish and builder.
  • Foundation: slab on grade $12,000–$28,000; full basement $45,000–$100,000+ (rarely justified under 800 sq ft).
  • Servicing: on a serviced lot this is modest; on an unserviced rural lot, well + septic + hydro + site work add $80,000–$150,000.
  • Permits: $2,000–$15,000 by construction value.
  • Development charges: $0–$60,000+, but exempt for qualifying garden suites under Bill 23.
  • Land: excluded from every figure above unless stated. Land is usually the largest single cost.

Rule of thumb from published Ontario data: a small prefab all-in commonly lands at $280,000–$420,000 excluding land once foundation, servicing, permits and charges are counted.
