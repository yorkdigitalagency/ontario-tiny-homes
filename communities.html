Public or potentially public

Arcadia Off-Grid Community — Commanda / South River Verify first

Off-grid land sale and lease community, about 3.5 hours north of Toronto. Solar power, rainwater collection, eco-focused. Published fees and property taxes: $300/month plus HST. Current public availability could not be independently confirmed — verify the operator, land interest, road access, utilities, occupancy rights and refund terms directly.

Tay Ridge — Perth Community

Community on the outskirts of Perth built around community building, affordability, sustainability, innovation and wellness. Confirm current availability and terms directly.

Affordable Tiny Homes Project — Marathon Under construction

Municipal small-home development with 19 planned homes, completion announced for late 2026. Confirm presale schedule, pricing and eligibility.

Junction Village Community Land Trust — Guelph Verify first

Established intentional multigenerational community and community land trust. It is not advertised as a conventional park with publicly available tiny-home pads — contact them about how membership and housing actually work.

Tiny Town, Craven Road — Toronto Not a joinable community

A one-way Toronto street famous for its collection of small homes in many styles, beside the city's longest wooden fence. It is a neighbourhood of small homes, not a community you buy into — watch Greenwood-Coxwell listings if you want to live there.

Bluewater Village — near Bayfield Verify first

Lakeside community described with walking trails, a clubhouse and a sustainability focus. Details are thin in public sources — verify existence, availability and terms before relying on it.

Almonte Tiny House Village — Almonte Verify first

Described with community gardens and sustainability programs. Details are thin in public sources — verify before relying on it.

Supportive housing — not open to the general public

Kingston Veterans' Village — Kingston Supportive only

Operating supportive tiny-home village. Housing and services are intended for eligible veterans experiencing a housing crisis.

Cabins on Ardelt (A Better Tent City) — Kitchener Supportive only

Operating supportive cabin community. It is supportive housing, not a pad-rental or homeownership development.

The Crossing — Peterborough Supportive only

50 individual modular units plus a service hub for people experiencing unsheltered homelessness. Not a public community.

Why so few?

Ontario has no province-wide tiny-home park system. Most legal tiny living happens one lot at a time — a backyard additional unit under municipal rules — rather than in named communities. That is why the Ontario rules guide matters more than this list for most buyers: the ADU route is open in far more places than any community directory.
